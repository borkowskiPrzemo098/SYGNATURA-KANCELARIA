# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS, GitHub Pages.

## Users

Business owners and private individuals in Poland with a legal problem (company disputes, contracts, real estate, inheritance, employment). They arrive stressed, often on the phone, and want to know quickly whether this firm handles their kind of case and how to make contact. Secondary audience: portfolio visitors judging whether the author can design in a serious register.

## Product Purpose

SYGNATURA is a fictional law firm (radcowie prawni i adwokaci, Poznań). The site presents practice areas, how cooperation works, the team and a contact form where the visitor picks the subject of the matter. Success = the visitor finds their area in seconds and sends a structured enquiry.

## Positioning

Named after "sygnatura akt", the case reference number: the firm presents itself through order, precision and clear procedure rather than marble-and-gavel cliché.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Practice areas, process steps, team, FAQ, contact form with topic choice (demo submit, no data sent).
- Lawyer names, figures and case results are fictional and must be labelled as a portfolio demo; no invented court victories presented as fact. Polish bar advertising rules: informative tone, no superlatives or promised outcomes.

## Product Principles

1. Calm and exact over impressive.
2. The visitor's problem first, the firm second.
3. Serious does not mean dull or thin: bold, readable type.
